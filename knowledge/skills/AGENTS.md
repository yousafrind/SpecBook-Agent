# Instructions for reusable engineering skills

- A skill must describe a repeatable task, not merely a topic.
- State its purpose, when to use it, required inputs, expected outputs, procedure, and verification.
- Identify tools or dependencies and provide alternatives where practical.
- Include constraints, failure cases, safety considerations, and escalation points.
- Keep skills scoped; compose small skills rather than creating one vague universal instruction.
- Do not claim a skill is executable or tested unless it has been exercised.
- Keep vendor-specific adapters separate from the underlying vendor-neutral procedure.
